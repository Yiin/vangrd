# Vangrd account access, removal, and support

You control who can view or manage your game account in Vangrd.
Account removal stops its automation. Support handles data questions and erasure requests.

## Shared account access

Owners can grant view or control access, change that access, and revoke it.
Payment access is separate. Support administrators have privileged access to help with account issues.

The [setup guide](https://vangrd.bot/guides/getting-started) explains account setup.
For agent sessions and handoffs, see [Agents and schedules](https://vangrd.bot/guides/schedules-and-safety).

## Account removal and data requests

Removing a game account ends its active automation and removes it from your active accounts.
Removal does not mean immediate erasure of every support, billing, or other retained record.

Use the in-app inbox to ask which records remain or request help with data erasure.

## Get help with a run or account

Open the inbox in [Vangrd](https://vangrd.bot/login) to report an issue or ask about your data.
For a failed run, include its time and the result you expected.
In **Status**, open the run's **Diagnostics** and use **Copy link to this run**.
The [Status guide](https://vangrd.bot/guides/travian-bot-status) shows where to find these details.

If a troop send has an unknown outcome, check the game before repeating it.
Use the [execution controls](execution-controls.md) to choose the stop that fits your situation.
